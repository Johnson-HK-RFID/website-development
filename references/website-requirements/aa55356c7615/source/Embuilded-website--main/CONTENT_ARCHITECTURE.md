## Services

### Field Engineering
Site survey, CCTV deployment, camera positioning, network design, PoE, 4G/5G, LoRaWAN, cabling, mounting, commissioning and maintenance.

### Connected Hardware + Engineering
Supply and integration of cameras, sensors, gateways, gas systems, HookCam, RFID and related field systems.

### Managed Device & Software Services
TRACI Cloud, lightweight PWA, device health, remote diagnostics, OTA, configuration, API, MQTT and webhook integration.
