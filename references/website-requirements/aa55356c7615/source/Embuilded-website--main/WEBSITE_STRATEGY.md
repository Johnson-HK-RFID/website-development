# Embuilded Intelligence Limited — Website Strategy

## Core positioning

**Embedded intelligence for the built world.**

Embuilded connects field devices, operational data and evidence into practical systems for construction, infrastructure and the wider built environment.

TRACI is the core technology platform. Embuilded also provides field engineering, connected hardware integration and managed device services.

## Brand hierarchy

- Embuilded Intelligence Limited — company
- TRACI — technology platform
- TRACI plugins — Safety, Vision, Asset, BCDS, SSSS
- TRACI devices and solutions — Gas, HookCam, Outrigger, Worker, Siren, RFID and related systems
- Embuilded Services — field engineering, systems integration and managed services

## Three commercial layers

1. Field Engineering Services
2. Connected Hardware + Engineering
3. Managed Device & Software Services

## Website audiences

- End users needing a complete solution
- Main contractors and built-world operators
- SSSS and 4S providers
- AI and CCTV partners
- System integrators

## Main message for channel partners

Keep your platform. Use our field engineering, connected devices and managed infrastructure underneath it.
